About

I turn what I observe into solutions that help people. I built Nubia Odyssey, an independent, Arabic-first digital archive of Nubian civilization, from a mobile phone, without a team or funding, because memory should not have to wait for an institution to deserve a place.

    Subproject · 2026

    Nubian Memory Network

    A family-memory register for Nubian villages, built inside the Nubia Odyssey project. Search a family name to read what the register holds, listen to Nubian words, follow the timeline of displacement, and add what your family knows.

    45villages in the register
    3tribal groups
    270candidate families, documentation in progress
    20references in the Studies Observatory
    • MapAn audio map of villages along the Nile, plus a 360° virtual tour.
    • DictionaryA talking Nubian word dictionary. Audio is experimental.
    • ToolsIn-browser OCR for photographed documents (experimental; Old Nubian support is limited), a photo-revive tool, and a 10-entry Manuscripts Lab.
    • ContributeA form for families and villagers to add names, stories and corrections.

    Status: prototype. Family records are marked “documentation in progress” until sources and family consent are in place.
